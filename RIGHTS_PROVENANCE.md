# Rights provenance and owner confirmation

On November 18, 2026, the owner confirmed that bc1q21 Retail and Professional code,
logo and custom stylesheet were created by him or by developers working on his behalf,
with the understanding that bc1q21 could freely use, modify and publicly distribute
their work. He directed preservation of Apache 2.0 for software and contributor
attribution, and preparation of the separate PDF and branding policies.

This is a record of the owner's representation and publication instructions. It is
not an independently verified copyright assignment, contributor agreement, trademark
registration, third-party permission or legal conclusion about ownership. No signed
assignments or complete chain-of-title documentation were independently reviewed in
this preparation. If a contributor's rights exceed that understanding, permission
from that rights holder may still be needed. The PDF's complete text/authorship and
font/document-source licensing have not been independently established by a contract.

Repository evidence:
- LICENSE carries the Apache 2.0 text selected to match Retail; NOTICE retains
  Dale Wells and Edgar Borja attribution. Valid existing Apache grants may support
  reuse without a new copyright assignment.
- Professional/assets/original/ASSET_PROVENANCE.json records exact historical source
  paths, Git blobs and hashes for the logo, custom stylesheet and Pico CSS. This
  establishes copied-file provenance, not ownership of every contribution.
- Third-party package notices retain their actual licenses. They are not relicensed
  by the owner confirmation. Runtime-notice inventory entries identify installed
  sources, versions and notice hashes, including nested package versions.
- The owner supplied and approved the standalone disclaimer PDF. Its bytes are
  preserved; its distribution permission is stated in ASSET_LICENSE.md. Microsoft
  font embedding flags indicate editable embedding and the Carlito/Caladea records
  identify OFL licensing, but technical flags alone do not establish lawful font
  acquisition. No standalone font software is distributed by this project.

The project is Apache-licensed software with separately governed non-code legal and
branding assets; it must not be described as granting Apache modification rights to
every file. Remaining legal uncertainties are recorded rather than declared resolved
by a successful checksum or software test. Historical privacy cleanup and any remote
publication remain separate authorization gates.
